## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an archival, museum-grade research environment that bridges the classical cryptological treatises of ancient India (such as the *Kautiliya Arthashastra* and the *Mlecchita Vikalpa*) with contemporary digital cybersecurity, computational logic, and cryptanalysis.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Esoteric yet Precision-Driven:** Evokes the sanctity of locked ancient vaults and the technical rigor of modern intelligence laboratories.
- **Scholarly & Authoritative:** Treats historical manuscripts not as decorative folklore, but as foundational computational algorithms.
- **Deep & Focused:** Reduces cognitive fatigue via an immersive, low-light workspace suited for dense textual decryptions, frequency analyses, and architectural diagramming.

### Visual Style
The design blends **Refined Dark Mode Minimalism** with **Technical Micro-Interfaces** and **Archival Manuscript Framing**:
- **Structured Obsidian Canvases:** Pure, ultra-low reflectance backdrops with subtle technical matrix coordinates and micro-grid indicators.
- **Cryptographic Filigree:** Hairline strokes (1px) finished in muted gold and oxidized copper that emulate both astronomical astrolabes and PCB traces.
- **Dual-Reality Signaling:** Warm, historical parchment/gold elements represent ancient ciphers, source shlokas, and manual transmutations; cold cyber-teal elements indicate automated decryption, live cryptographic states, entropy analysis, and modern code execution.

## Colors

The color architecture enforces a strict epistemic dichotomy between historical artifact and computational readout.

### Key Palettes
- **Primary — Antique Gold (`#D6AD60`):** Used for primary semantic weight, classical scholarly milestones, illuminated structural accents, primary CTAs, and active historical tabs.
- **Secondary — Cyber Teal (`#4FD1C5`):** Reserved strictly for modern cryptographic feedback, terminal telemetry, byte-stream visualizers, entropy meters, and real-time computation states.
- **Tertiary — Copper Bronze (`#A66B45`):** Employed for tertiary navigation, manuscript commentary, secondary boundaries, and seal stamps.
- **Neutral — Obsidian Black (`#090B0F`):** Deep foundation canvas ensuring pure contrast ratios for dense analytical viewing.

### Supporting Canvas & Typography Tones
- **Surface Elevation (`#141820`):** Charcoal layer for panels, cards, and floating analysis drawers.
- **Surface Border Accent (`#262D3D`):** Low-contrast structural framing with directional gold washes.
- **Primary Text — Bleached Parchment (`#F3F0E9`):** 16.2:1 contrast ratio against Obsidian Black for optimal legibility.
- **Secondary Text — Atmospheric Slate (`#A8ADB7`):** Metadata, axis labels, script transliterations, and inactive controls.
- **Artifact Parchment (`#E9DDC3`):** Highlighting authentic primary-source extracts, folio callouts, and Vedic mathematical lemmas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a high-contrast triad between classical humanism, ergonomic functional UI, and strict monospaced computation.

### Roles & Allocations
- **EB Garamond (Display & Section Headlines):** Delivers literary gravitas, historical authority, and intellectual poise for epigraphs, cipher names, and analytical chapter titles.
- **Manrope (Interface UI & Discourse):** Provides geometric clarity, neutral transparency, and high legibility across analytical bodies, research annotations, and control layouts.
- **JetBrains Mono (Cipher Terminals, Matrices & Labels):** Renders all encrypted strings, binary representations, mathematical transliterations, Ka-Ta-Pa-Ya-Di mapping tables, and system telemetry labels.
- **Indic Script Support (Noto Serif Devanagari):** Serves authentic Sanskrit/Prakrit original codices alongside English analytical breakdowns, harmonizing organically in weight and baseline with EB Garamond.

### Stylistic Directives
- Display serif headers must always be rendered in sentence or title case—never all-caps—to preserve the graceful cadence of literary type.
- All micro-labels, metadata markers, and terminal badges use uppercase JetBrains Mono with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine operates on an 8px base rhythm, structured within an adaptable multi-column laboratory grid.

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid with 48px outer margins and 24px gutters. Workspaces accommodate an asymmetric split: a 5-column archival source panel (manuscript imagery, Sanskrit shlokas, historical provenance) flanking a 7-column interactive cipher lab (matrix transformations, algorithm logs, deciphering terminals).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 32px outer margins and 16px gutters. Panels fold into tabbed, synced split views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px outer margins and 12px gutters. Interfaces stack vertically with an anchored micro-dock for switching between source cipher and computational workspace.

### Spatial Discipline
- Maintain strict boundary paddings across panels using `space-lg`.
- Group inline telemetry (e.g., hash indicators, substitution keys) using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Isolate disparate analytical modules with `space-xl` to avoid clutter in data-dense interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, natural drop shadows; instead, it relies on structural luminosity, surface reflectivity, and hairline borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas — `#090B0F`):** The foundational substrate, layered with faint mathematical grid intersections spaced at 64px intervals (`rgba(214, 173, 96, 0.04)`).
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Cards — `#141820`):** Elevated research containers with a 1px solid border (`#262D3D`).
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules & Floating Modals — `#1A202C`):** Higher-level interactive panels featuring a 1px composite border: top and left borders highlighted with `rgba(214, 173, 96, 0.35)` to simulate an overhead directional key light.

### Glassmorphism & Cryptographic Overlays
Floating analysis overlays, deciphering HUDs, and code inspector sheets use background blur:
- **Properties:** `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background-color: rgba(20, 24, 32, 0.82);`
- **Border Treatment:** Hairline border of `rgba(79, 209, 197, 0.25)` when active modern compute is running, or `rgba(214, 173, 96, 0.25)` during archival contemplation.
- **Ambient Focus Glow:** Focused cryptanalysis keys and decrypted characters emit a tight, controlled outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(79, 209, 197, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, disciplined geometry reflecting the precision of chiselled stone inscriptions, copper-plate charters, and silicon wafers.

### Radius System
- **Level 1 (Soft — Base Radius: 4px):** Applied to cards, input fields, interactive terminal blocks, and buttons. Maintains architectural crispness while avoiding raw brutalism.
- **Nested Inner Radii:** Elements inside panels (such as cipher token pills or terminal keys) use an inner radius of 2px (`rounded-xs`) to maintain geometric parallelism with the outer 4px parent.
- **Precision Corners:** Corner tick marks (2×2px gold L-brackets) are positioned on high-priority analytical cards to reinforce the aesthetic of laboratory equipment and archival specimen holders.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Historical Action (e.g., "Synthesize Permutation"):** Background in Antique Gold (`#D6AD60`), text in `#090B0F` (Semi-bold Manrope), 4px border radius. Hover state: `#E5BE72` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(214, 173, 96, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Cryptographic Action (e.g., "Execute Decryption"):** Background in transparent charcoal (`#141820`), 1px solid border in Cyber Teal (`#4FD1C5`), text in `#4FD1C5` (JetBrains Mono). Hover state: `background: rgba(79, 209, 197, 0.1)`.
- **Archival Text Action:** Ghost button with an underline stroke in Copper Bronze (`#A66B45`) and uppercase tracked font.

### Cipher Chips & Key Tokens
- Individual characters or syllables (e.g., Aksharas or hexadecimal pairs) sit within dedicated micro-cells:
  - Default: `#141820` fill, 1px `#262D3D` border, `#F3F0E9` JetBrains Mono text.
  - Active/Shifted: `#1A202C` fill, 1px `#4FD1C5` border, `#4FD1C5` text with subtle inner luminescence.
  - Ancient Anchor: `#1A202C` fill, 1px `#D6AD60` border, `#D6AD60` text.

### Input Fields & Terminal Consoles
- **Console Input:** Flat Obsidian base (`#090B0F`) inset into Charcoal panels. Border is 1px solid `#262D3D`, transitioning to 1px `#4FD1C5` on focus.
- **Prefix Indicators:** Command prompts utilize the Antique Gold `›` glyph or Devanagari numerals.
- **Font Stack:** All input fields render exclusively in `JetBrains Mono` to preserve column alignment in multiline cipher arrays.

### Research Cards & Folio Panels
- Outlined in `#262D3D` with an optional top border accent line in Antique Gold (`#D6AD60`).
- Card headers feature an archival index tag in the top right corner (e.g., `FOLIO // 084`, `CIPHER.V1`) styled in `label-caps` typography with Slate text (`#A8ADB7`).

### Checkboxes, Radio Controls & Toggles
- Custom square controls with 2px corner radii.
- Checked state fills with `#D6AD60` featuring an Obsidian Black checkmark, or fills with `#4FD1C5` when inside digital decrypter toolbars.

### Specialized Lab Components
- **Frequency Spectrum Visualizer:** Bar charts charting letter distributions rendered in `#4FD1C5` against background benchmark curves in `#A66B45`.
- **Manuscript Transcription Drawer:** High-resolution illuminated palm-leaf viewer with synced interlinear digital transliterations and script substitution matrix overlays.
