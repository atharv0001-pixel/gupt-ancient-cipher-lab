last_page संयुक्तव्यञ्जनेषु परमेव

Historical Epistemological Reality Check

The Katapayādi system was formulated for mnemonic immortality, not contemporary cryptographic adversarial secrecy. Because Sanskrit vowels do not alter consonant-assigned integers, mathematicians could construct multi-layered devotional poetry (such as verses extolling Krishna or Shiva) that simultaneously transmitted sine tables, lunar cycles, and transcendental formulas across millennia without manuscript degradation.

Āryabhaṭīya Gitikā Pada Āryabhaṭa I (499 CE)

Unlike Katapayādi where vowels are ignored, Āryabhaṭa assigned powers of 100 to vowels (अ=10⁰, इ=10², उ=10⁴) while consonants (वर्ग & अवर्ग) represented numbers 1–25 and 30–100.
